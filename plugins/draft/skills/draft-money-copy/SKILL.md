# Writing about money for a founder

Draft keeps its rules for this copy in the brain, and they change. This skill holds none of them.

Before you write or review a word of it, search the brain connector for "money copy rules", fetch the record it points to, and follow it exactly, along with draft-writing. Cite the record you used.

If the brain doesn't answer, or you can't find that record, write nothing about money, credit, costs or terms. Tell the person you couldn't reach the rules, and leave the money parts of the copy for when you can.
